**Currency:** GBP

## A1.1 Effective Date

A1.1.1 This Amendment takes effect on 1 January 2025.

A1.1.2 This Amendment applies **by Service Date**. A line item whose Service Date falls on or after the effective date is priced under this Amendment; a line item whose Service Date falls before the effective date is priced under Appendix B. The date on which an invoice is issued is irrelevant for this purpose.
