## 1. Parties, Term and Structure

1.1 This Base Agreement is made between Meridian Health Assurance Group (the "Payer") and Rivermead General Hospital (the "Provider").

1.2 The term runs from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2025.

1.3 This Base Agreement is to be read together with Appendix B (Rate Schedule) and with any amendment executed under clause 1.4. In the event of conflict, an amendment prevails over Appendix B, and Appendix B prevails over this Base Agreement.

1.4 The Parties may amend the rates and the list of contracted Services by a numbered amendment executed by both Parties. An amendment states its own effective date and the basis on which that date operates.

1.5 The Provider delivers the Services from Main Campus (F-MAIN). No facility differential applies, and all plan tiers are reimbursed identically.

## 2. Interpretation

2.1 "Service Day" means the calendar day recorded as the Service Date of the line item.

2.2 "Business Day" means any Service Day other than a Saturday or a Sunday.

2.3 "Episode of Care" means the continuous period from a Patient's admission to that Patient's discharge. An Episode of Care may span more than one Service Day.

2.4 "Unit" means one billable unit of a Service on the unit basis recorded for that Service in Appendix B.

## 3. Calculation Conventions

3.1 All monetary amounts are expressed in whole cents. Where the application of a multiplier, premium or discount produces a fraction of a cent, the result shall be rounded to the nearest whole cent, with exact halves rounded away from zero ("half up"). Rounding is applied after each individual step of the calculation, not once at the end.

3.2 Where more than one adjustment applies to the same Service, the adjustments shall be applied to the base rate strictly in the following order: (a) substitution of a bundled rate; (b) the facility multiplier; (c) the plan-tier multiplier; (d) any premium or uplift; and (e) any cumulative volume discount. The line total is then the resulting unit rate multiplied by the billed quantity.

3.3 The line total is the effective unit rate multiplied by the billed quantity; the invoice total is the sum of the line totals.
