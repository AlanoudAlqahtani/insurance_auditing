## Article I — Recitals and Term

1.1 This Agreement is made between Meridian Health Assurance Group (the "Payer") and St. Auben Metropolitan Hospital Trust (the "Provider").

1.2 This Agreement takes effect on 1 January 2024 and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, expires on 31 December 2025.

1.3 The Provider delivers the Services from Main Campus (F-MAIN). No facility differential applies under this Agreement, and the rates set out in the Articles below apply irrespective of the Patient's plan tier.

1.4 The rates for individual Services are not gathered into a single schedule. Each rate appears in the Article in which the relevant Service is described, and the Articles are not ordered by clinical speciality.

## Article II — Definitions and Interpretation

2.1 In this Agreement the following expressions have the following meanings.

2.2 "Service Day" means the period of twenty-four (24) hours commencing at 07:00 hours and concluding at 06:59 hours on the following calendar day. A Service delivered wholly within a single calendar day is delivered on the Service Day bearing that calendar date.

2.3 "Service Date" means the date recorded against a line item on the Provider's invoice in respect of the Service to which that line item relates.

2.4 "Business Day" means a Service Day which commences on a day other than a Saturday or a Sunday.

2.5 "Episode of Care" means the continuous period beginning with a Patient's admission and ending with that Patient's discharge, including any period of leave of absence of less than forty-eight (48) hours. An Episode of Care may span more than one Service Day and more than one calendar month.

2.6 "Unit" means one billable unit of a Service on the unit basis stated for that Service in the Article in which its rate appears.

2.7 "Cumulative utilisation" means the running total of Units of a Service billed under this Agreement, taken in Service Date order, up to but excluding the line item being priced.

2.8 References to an Article are references to an Article of this Agreement. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect construction.

## Article III — Calculation Conventions

3.1 All monetary amounts are expressed in whole cents. Where the application of a multiplier, premium or discount produces a fraction of a cent, the result shall be rounded to the nearest whole cent, with exact halves rounded away from zero ("half up"). Rounding is applied after each individual step of the calculation, not once at the end.

3.2 Where more than one adjustment applies to the same Service, the adjustments shall be applied to the base rate strictly in the following order: (a) substitution of a bundled rate; (b) the facility multiplier; (c) the plan-tier multiplier; (d) any premium or uplift; and (e) any cumulative volume discount. The line total is then the resulting unit rate multiplied by the billed quantity.

3.3 The line total for a line item is the effective unit rate for that line item multiplied by the quantity billed. The invoice total is the arithmetic sum of the line totals appearing on that invoice, and no other amount.

3.4 A threshold expressed by reference to a Service Day is assessed against the aggregate quantity of the Service delivered to the Patient on that Service Day, and not against the quantity appearing on any single line item.

3.5 A cumulative volume discount applies to a line item where cumulative utilisation of the Service prior to that line item exceeds the stated threshold. Utilisation is counted in Service Date order, and where two line items share a Service Date they are counted in ascending order of line identifier. Where more than one threshold has been exceeded, the deeper discount applies and the discounts are not compounded.

3.6 An exclusion window is measured in either direction from the Service Date of the Service which is excluded.
