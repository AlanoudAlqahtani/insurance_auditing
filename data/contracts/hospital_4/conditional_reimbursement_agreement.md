## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Service Day" means the calendar day recorded as the Service Date of the line item.

1.2 "Business Day" means any Service Day other than a Saturday or a Sunday.

1.3 "Unit" means one billable unit of a Service on the unit basis recorded for that Service in Section 3.

1.4 "Instance" means one Unit. The two expressions are used interchangeably in this Agreement.

1.5 "Adjustment" means any multiplier, premium, uplift, discount or substituted rate provided for in Sections 5 to 9 inclusive.

## 2. Term and Scope

2.1 This Agreement runs from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2025 between Meridian Health Assurance Group and Calderwood University Teaching Hospital.

2.2 All Services are delivered from Main Campus (F-MAIN); no facility differential arises under this Agreement and plan tier does not affect the rate.

2.3 A rate stated in Section 3 is a base rate and is never the amount payable until every applicable Adjustment has been applied in the order set out in Section 4.

## 4. Order of Adjustments

4.1 Where more than one adjustment applies to the same Service, the adjustments shall be applied to the base rate strictly in the following order: (a) substitution of a bundled rate; (b) the facility multiplier; (c) the plan-tier multiplier; (d) any premium or uplift; and (e) any cumulative volume discount. The line total is then the resulting unit rate multiplied by the billed quantity.

4.2 All monetary amounts are expressed in whole cents. Where the application of a multiplier, premium or discount produces a fraction of a cent, the result shall be rounded to the nearest whole cent, with exact halves rounded away from zero ("half up"). Rounding is applied after each individual step of the calculation, not once at the end.

4.3 Adjustments are applied successively and are not compounded within a single class. In particular, where two thresholds in Section 8 are exceeded, only the deeper discount is applied; the two are not multiplied together.

4.4 The line total is the effective unit rate produced by this Section multiplied by the quantity billed. The invoice total is the sum of the line totals on the invoice and nothing else.
